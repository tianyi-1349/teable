## Summary

VoltAgent's source style in `awesome-design-md` is a dark, AI-native, tool-oriented interface language built around sharp grouping, technical confidence, and a tightly controlled green accent.

## Key Signals Preserved For This Repo

- AI-native, sharp, technical presentation
- Structured cards and crisp boundaries
- Compact, highly scannable hierarchy
- Strong operator-oriented clarity
- Controlled accent emphasis for active and important states

## Local Adaptation Notes

- In this repo, VoltAgent style is used as a workflow-product influence, not a dramatic landing-page aesthetic.
- We use it primarily for `dashboard`, `workflow`, and configuration-dense surfaces.
- We do not import aggressive glow effects or theatrical marketing motion into production product pages.
